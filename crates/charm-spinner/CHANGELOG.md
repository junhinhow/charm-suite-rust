## [0.1.0]

### Added

- `Spinner` type with the `frames` and `interval` fields, the `new` constructor
  and the `render` method (cycling with wrap-around, exactly like the Go
  `Render`).
- The 30 predefined spinners as functions: `dots()`, `line()`, `star()`,
  `moon()`, `earth()`, `clock()`, `bounce()`, `bar()`, `circle()`, `square()`,
  `triangle()`, `arrow()`, `pulse()`, `flip()`, `grow()`, `layer()`, `toggle()`,
  `balloon()`, `noise()`, `fading()`, `christmas()`, `monkey()`, `hearts()`,
  `weather()`, `soccer()`, `matrix()`, `dna()`, `orbit()`, `rocket()` and
  `fire()`.
- `all()` returning every predefined spinner in a `BTreeMap<&'static str, Spinner>`,
  keyed by the same names used by the Go implementation.
- `DEFAULT_STYLE` and `default_style()`, the counterpart of the Go package-level
  `var Style = lipgloss.NewStyle()`.
- `scripts/generate_spinners.rs`, the generator that extracts every frame from
  `reference/go/charm-spinner/spinner.go` programmatically. The generated
  `src/spinners.rs` stores the frames as `\u{...}` escapes, keeping the source
  pure ASCII and immune to mojibake.
- Parity golden files generated by the Go original
  (`reference/go/charm-spinner/cmd/parity`), including a raw byte dump
  (`spinners_dump.txt`) that proves the 204 frames are byte-identical to the Go
  implementation.
- Runnable demo in `examples/demo.rs`.
- English and Portuguese (`pt-BR`) documentation.
