## [0.1.0]

### Added

- `Profile` with the public fields `no_color`, `dumb`, `high_contrast`,
  `reduced_motion`, `screen_reader`, `unicode` and `width`, and `Default` for the
  zero value (as the Go zero value).
- `Profile::detect`, `Profile::detect_from` and `Profile::default_profile`, the
  Rust counterparts of Go's `Detect`, `DetectFrom` and `Default`. Detection
  follows the Go rules for `NO_COLOR`, `CLICOLOR`, `TERM=dumb`, `ASCII`,
  `A11Y_ASCII`, `A11Y_HIGH_CONTRAST`, `A11Y_REDUCED_MOTION`,
  `A11Y_SCREEN_READER` and `COLUMNS`.
- `Profile::describe` and `Display`, the stable one-line summary used by
  `--version` and debug output.
- `Profile::symbol`, `Profile::frames` and `Profile::announce`.
- `Profile::sanitize`, `Profile::style` and `Profile::border`, which degrade
  lipgloss styles and borders for the profile.
- `strip_ansi` and `transliterate` as free functions.
- The ASCII transliteration table (222 glyphs) extracted programmatically from
  `ascii.go`.
- Ported unit tests, doctests and a parity suite with a golden file generated by
  `reference/go/charm-a11y/cmd/parity`.
- Runnable demo in `examples/charm_a11y_demo.rs`.
- English and Portuguese (`pt-BR`) documentation.
